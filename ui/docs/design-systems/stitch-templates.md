# ARX Stitch Design-System Templates

Stitch project: `projects/5652752859028545787`

The live Stitch project was created for ARX dashboard UI exploration. These
templates are written as Stitch-ready design briefs and are mirrored in git so
engineers and AI agents can audit the visual intent without depending on external
project state.

## 1. Capital Command

Default implementation.

- Audience: AI SaaS founder reviewing their ARX score and advance amount.
- Personality: premium, decisive, technical, high-trust.
- Palette: near-black `#05070d`, navy panels `#0d1626`, electric blue `#2563eb`,
  cyan `#38bdf8`, green `#22c55e`, amber `#f59e0b`, red `#ef4444`.
- Typography: Inter, bold numeric hierarchy, compact dashboard labels.
- Shape: 8px cards, pill badges, round primary CTA.
- Layout: score and simulator prominent, table dense, signal bars direct.
- Best for: demo-ready founder dashboard and pitch polish.

## 2. Credit Ops Console

- Audience: internal ARX underwriting and audit users.
- Personality: calm, dense, compliance-aware, operational.
- Palette: light slate background, white panels, navy text, restrained blue
  highlights, risk colors only for status.
- Typography: Inter, smaller table-first hierarchy.
- Shape: 6px cards, squared inputs, compact controls.
- Layout: table and filters dominate, scorecard secondary.
- Best for: NightFury20X/Kiran audit and finance review workflows.

## 3. Signal Radar

- Audience: founder or analyst trying to understand what changed.
- Personality: analytical, chart-forward, diagnostic.
- Palette: dark graphite, cyan line charts, blue primary score, amber risk trend.
- Typography: Inter, strong section labels, chart annotations.
- Shape: 8px cards, thin borders, minimal shadows.
- Layout: trend chart and five-signal breakdown above company table.
- Best for: explaining behavioral intelligence as the ARX moat.

## 4. Boardroom Intelligence

- Audience: judges, investors, mentors, and executive demos.
- Personality: polished, restrained, boardroom-grade.
- Palette: navy header, white workspace, dark text, blue accents, status chips.
- Typography: Inter, larger executive metrics, moderate whitespace.
- Shape: 8px cards, crisp table grid, low-shadow panels.
- Layout: narrative top strip, KPI row, selected company story, simulator.
- Best for: Startup Wednesday presentation and investor walkthroughs.

## 5. Data Flow Narrative

- Audience: technical reviewers validating how ARX data becomes a score.
- Personality: transparent, systems-oriented, explainable.
- Palette: light workspace, blue connector nodes, green scoring results, amber
  Airtable storage, navy code/config blocks.
- Typography: Inter with monospace labels for fields and adapters.
- Shape: 8px flow nodes, thin borders, compact cards.
- Layout: data-flow diagram first, then dashboard rendering and simulator.
- Best for: explaining Anish/Kiran/Vaibhav ownership boundaries.
